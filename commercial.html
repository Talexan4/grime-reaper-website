Commercial Services

First impressions matter. The Grime Reaper Power Washing Co. provides professional commercial cleaning services throughout Oklahoma City and the surrounding areas. From parking lots and storefronts to building exteriors and fleet vehicles, we help businesses maintain a clean, professional image year-round.

  • Parking Lot Cleaning

    Parking lot cleaning removes oil stains, dirt, and debris to create a cleaner, safer, and more professional appearance for your business.

  • Storefront Washing

    Storefront washing removes dirt, grime, and buildup from entrances and windows to help your business make a strong first impression.

  • Building Exterior Washing

    Exterior building washing removes dirt, mold, and buildup that can damage surfaces and make your property look worn over time

  • Trash Can Cleaning

    Commercial trash can cleaning removes odors, bacteria, and buildup to keep your business area clean and sanitary

  • Fleet Vehicle Washing

    Fleet vehicle washing keeps company vehicles clean, professional, and well-maintained, helping represent your business property
